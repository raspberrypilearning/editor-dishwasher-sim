## Make it your own

Make the dishwasher simulator your own.

<h2 class="c-project-heading--explainer">What you need to do</h2>

Choose one idea to try:

- Can you add a timer so that the player can see how many dishes they can clean in one minute?
- Can you add a new dish sprite with six dirty-to-clean costumes and add its message to the `stuff`{:class="block3variables"} list?
- Can you make the dishes get dirty again if the player waits too long?

## Now run your code

Play the game from the beginning after each change. Check that dishes can still be cleaned and put away, the score increases, and the music controls work.

Remember to save your project.
